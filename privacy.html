Privacy Policy

Soft Hours Haven (the "Channel") and its private publishing tool "Content OS" (the "App"). Last updated: October 2026.

What the App is

Content OS is a private tool used by a single creator, the owner of the Soft Hours Haven YouTube channel, to upload her own videos to her own channel and to read the channel's own performance statistics. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Google user data the App accesses

When the owner authorizes the App with her Google account, the App requests only these permissions:

  • youtube.upload: to upload videos and thumbnails to the owner's own channel.
  • youtube.readonly: to confirm which channel the authorization belongs to and to read the status of the owner's own videos.
  • yt-analytics.readonly: to read the owner's own channel analytics (for example watch time and views).

How the data is used

The data is used only to publish the owner's videos and to show her how her own channel is performing. It is not sold, shared, rented, transferred to third parties, or used for advertising. It is not used to train machine-learning models.

Storage and security

The authorization token is stored encrypted in the owner's own private accounts and is used only by the App. No personal data about viewers or any third party is collected. Statistics are stored only to measure the owner's own channel.

Retention and revoking access

The owner can revoke the App's access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. After revocation the App can no longer access any Google data.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The App's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the comments or the About tab of the Soft Hours Haven YouTube channel.
